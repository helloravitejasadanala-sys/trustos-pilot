# TrustOS Claude Design Pack

## Read in this order

1. **01_TrustOS_Product_Handbook.pdf** — product truth, mission, users, lifecycle and phase boundaries.
2. **02_TrustOS_App_Prototype_v3.2.html** — broad interactive product reference. Use it to understand roles, workflows and required states. Do not copy its interface literally.
3. **03_TrustOS_Public_Website.html** — approved brand and emotional direction: warm, calm, premium and human. Use its identity, not its marketing-page layout.
4. **References/** — individual visual ideas for Today, dashboard hierarchy and project-workspace composition.

## Important source priority

When sources conflict:

1. Product Handbook controls product truth and phase scope.
2. The v3.2 prototype controls workflow coverage and screen/state requirements.
3. The public website controls brand emotion and visual character.
4. The small mockups are inspiration only.

## Do not combine everything into one page

Design three connected experiences within one system:

- Internal TrustOS Admin Console
- Vendor Workspace
- Client Portal

The vendor app must share one shell across professions and adapt through terminology, project type, Event Details, preparation and delivery — not through separate applications.

## What not to copy

- Do not copy the handbook layout into the product.
- Do not copy the website hero/layout into the operational app.
- Do not copy every screen or feature from v3.2 without checking Phase 1 scope.
- Do not use Blueprint v4 as the primary interface reference. It is placed in Optional Archive only for strategy context.

## Signature product behaviours

- **Do This First** — the highest-priority action across the vendor's work.
- **One Clear Next Action** — the next action within a specific project.
- One secure client link.
- Event Details instead of a generic questionnaire.
- Manual-first pilot: free collaboration and manual payment must work without Stripe.

## Deliverable requested from Claude

Create a coherent high-fidelity design system and clickable prototype for desktop and mobile. Include authentication, empty workspace, Today, projects, clients, project workflow, client portal, payments, messages, delivery, and internal admin monitoring. Preserve product logic while replacing generic AI-SaaS visual patterns.
