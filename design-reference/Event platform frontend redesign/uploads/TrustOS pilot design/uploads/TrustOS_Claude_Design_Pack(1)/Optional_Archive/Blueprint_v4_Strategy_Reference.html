Section 01

Five pillars, one cycle

The pillars are not a list of capabilities. They are a loop, and Learning is where it turns.

Trust

Nobody can dispute what was agreed, sent, signed or paid.

Workflow

Nobody ever wonders what happens next.

Preparation

Avoidable mistakes are handled before the day.

Delivery

The project continues until both sides consider it complete.

Learning

Every completed project makes the next one easier.

Learning creates Operational Intelligence → which feeds Trust, because you can prove what you know → which improves Workflow, because certainty removes hesitation → which improves Preparation → which improves Delivery → which generates Learning.

Section 03

Operational Intelligence

Promoted in v4.0 from future feature to Phase 1 core. Phase 1 collects data. That is all Phase 1 does — no AI, no recommendations, no community.

Phase 1

Venue

Access, parking, power, internet, light, restrictions, staff.

Phase 1

Client

Communication style, decision pattern, sensitivities, family context.

Phase 1

Equipment

What was needed, what was missing, what failed.

Phase 1

Communication

What worked, what caused confusion, what timing landed.

Phase 1

Workflow

Process friction, sequencing, timing.

Phase 1

General

Everything else. Reviewed quarterly for emerging patterns.

The decision that matters most: a note must attach to a durable entity — a venue, a client, an equipment item — not only to a project. A note attached to a project is a diary entry. A note attached to Coombe Abbey surfaces automatically the next time anyone works there. One foreign key now; a rewrite later.

Section 05

Network effect roadmap

Four stages. Each earns the next with written evidence. Nothing auto-advances — skipping to stage 3 because it is the exciting part is the most likely way this product dies.

1

Collect Phase 1

Every completed project deposits a structured note against a venue, client or equipment item. Honest position: this asks for effort and returns almost nothing yet. It must be fast.

2

Search Phase 1 late / 2

Notes surface automatically for a known venue or client. A database query, not intelligence — and most of the practical benefit.

3

Recommend Phase 3

Patterns from the vendor's own history, always shown with source and confidence. Never as a guarantee.

4

Shared intelligence Long term

Consented, anonymised, cross-vendor venue knowledge. The moat — and the legal minefield. Venue notes may one day be shared. Client notes never will be.

Section 06

Pricing — documented, not built

No billing code in Phase 1. These are hypotheses to test with real vendors, not decisions. Annual billing is two months free.

Starter · £15/mo

5 active projects · 1 user · full client portal · contracts, signature, deposits · Operational Intelligence included.

Professional · £29/mo

Unlimited projects · 3 team members · equipment and crew workflow · templates when they arrive.

Business · £59/mo

10 team members · multi-brand · priority support. Enterprise later, on request.

Never gate the Learning pillar. Operational Intelligence appears on every tier including the cheapest. It is the reason to stay and it compounds — gating it destroys the moat to win a short-term upgrade. TrustOS also takes no percentage of vendor revenue.
