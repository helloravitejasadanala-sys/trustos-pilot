# Claude Design Instruction

Use the files in this pack as source material. Read `READ_ME_FIRST.md` before opening the other files.

You are the Founding Product Designer and Creative Director for TrustOS. Design a coherent Phase 1 pilot experience for MiniMomentz that is calm, premium, creative, human and usable without training by a non-technical 45–60-year-old vendor or client.

Do not merely polish the existing prototype. Audit it, preserve the product truth, remove confusion, and redesign the operational experience.

The system contains three connected experiences:

1. Internal TrustOS Admin Console
2. Vendor Workspace
3. Client Portal

Use one shared design system. The Vendor Workspace adapts to photography, live streaming, DJ, makeup, decor and event planning through content and workflow — not separate dashboard products.

The two signature behaviours must be unmistakable:

- **Do This First** on Today: the single highest-priority action across active projects.
- **One Clear Next Action** inside a project: one current stage, responsible person, clear action and one dominant CTA.

Use the approved public website for emotional direction: warm editorial utility, paper-like neutrals, forest green, calm typography and restrained motion. Do not use its hero layout inside the app.

The Client Portal must feel like a guided concierge page, not a dashboard. No account, one secure link, one current action, only relevant project information.

Create:

- Design rationale and emotional direction
- Design tokens and component rules
- Desktop and mobile clickable prototype
- Auth and empty-workspace states
- Today
- Projects and Clients
- New project and add-client flow
- Project Workspace and lifecycle states
- Event Details
- Quote and acceptance
- Manual payment, Stripe-ready and free collaboration states
- Preparation, messages, completion, delivery and approval
- Client Portal
- Internal Admin Console
- Loading, empty, error, offline, success, waiting and archived states
- A `DESIGN.md` engineering handoff

Avoid generic AI SaaS patterns, giant marketing headings, decorative gradients, excessive empty space, random icons, glassmorphism and vague button labels.

Every screen must make clear:

- Who is using it?
- What has happened?
- What is waiting?
- What should they do next?
- What happens after the action?

Do not add unsupported Phase 2 features. Do not change product strategy. Return the working design files and a concise engineering handoff.
